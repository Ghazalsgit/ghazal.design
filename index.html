Unwrappa

A marketplace where creators and brands find each other. I met a small creator who could not get collaborations. She was good, brands just had no way to find her.

  • 1Matching goes both ways. Brands can invite creators they want, and creators can apply to brands themselves, so a collaboration can start from either side instead of over cold DMs.
  • 2Creators are verified manually before they can apply. Slower to grow, but it means a brand can trust who is reaching out to them.
  • 3500 creators and 14 brands today, on recurring monthly revenue that is still growing. I run the growth myself and ship improvements most days.
  • 4Mine from the first idea to launch: the problem, the product decisions, the design, and the marketing.

Mediakitser

Media kits any creator can make in minutes. Running Unwrappa meant looking at media kits all day, and the pattern was obvious: the creators without one were not getting collaborations at all.

  • 1Pick a template, fill in your numbers, download. It replaces an afternoon in a design tool, so a creator can have a good kit whether or not they know how to design.
  • 2Built as its own product rather than a feature inside Unwrappa. Every creator needs it, including the ones who will never join a marketplace.
  • 3Free to preview, paid to download. Around two sales a week, with all the marketing done by me.
  • 4My second product, started while the first was running and taken from idea to launch on my own initiative.
